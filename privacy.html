Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the apps published by Purple Rootbeer Partners Limited Partnership ("Purple Rootbeer," "we," "us") on the Apple App Store, and this website. Questions? Email support@purplerootbeer.com.

What we collect

Our apps are built to work without an account, and we do not collect personal information unless you choose to give it to us. If you email us, we keep your email address and message so we can help you. If a specific app collects anything else, such as anonymous crash reports, we will describe it in that app's App Store privacy details.

Purchases

Apple processes all App Store purchases and subscriptions. We never see or store your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy covers that information.

How we use information

We use what we collect only to answer your questions, fix problems, and improve our apps. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, except service providers that help us run our apps under confidentiality terms, or when the law requires it.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete any information we have about you. Email support@purplerootbeer.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date.
